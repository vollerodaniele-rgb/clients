Adding a client

1. Copy data/_example.json to data/<client>.json and fill it in.
2. Copy the three files in _template/ into a new folder named <client>/.
3. Commit. It appears here on its own, live at clients.noiraunoir.com/<client>/.

No new repo, no DNS record, no Pages setting, no relay change, and this same key opens it.
